Activities of Foundation

  • Kutch is encircled by the barren Rann on one side and the sea on the other. It is known for perpetual scarcity. It is being constantly squeezed by frequent famines, so the ground water level is depleting very fast. Hence more and more villages are becoming abandoned. Salinity ingress is major problem and ground water is getting saline. We all know that the villages are the backbone of economic development of the agricultural country like India. So we have to give priority to the rural development for the national prosperity. In this context, the migration of people from the villages to cities in search of work creates many problems, like

  • 1 Imbalance in the density of the population

    2 Proliferation of slums

    3 Problems of health and hygiene

    4 Problems of unemployment

    5 Problems of social unrest and loosing of identity

    6 Moreover, Kutch being a border area needs people in the villages particularly around the boarder areas for safety

    7 Therefore it is absolutely necessary to make these villagers self-reliant.

Water Harvesting

  • Water is harvested in the following ways, by creation/construction Staggered Trenches Loose Boulders Nala Plugs Storage Tanks Check Dams Recharging Check Dams Recharging Storage Tanks Khet Talavdies (Farm Ponds) Recharging of Wells Bori Bandhs (Creating obstruction by placing used bags filled with sand)

Agricultural Development

  • Following activities are carried out in the field of Agricultural development Making water available to farmers with the help of measures described above. Distribution of improved Seeds to framers at subsidized rates Promoting horticulture Organizing Training and Tours for education of farmers in latest agricultural techniques Promotion of Organic farming

Animal Care

  • Making arrangement for drinking water for animal Cattle Feed Programme Vaccination Programme In times of draughts free distribution of fodder to feed animals

Women and Child Care

  • Distribution of Smokeless Choolahs Making the women self dependent by arranging the Self Help Groups Women health care activities Kitchen Garden Child heath care activities

Employment to Local People

  • For each work carried out by Ashapura Foundation the workers are arranged from the project area where the work is to be carried out. The objective behind this is to provide employment to the local people at their door step.

Promotion of Education

  • Education is promoted by following activities Distribution of study material Prize distribution for encouragement School Sanitation Tutorial Classes Qualitative and quantitative improvement of primary education

Relief and Rehabilitation

  • Ashapura Foundation's role in disasters like earthquake, cyclone, drought etc. is by way of Providing immediate relief to the affected people Rehabilitation of affected people

Rural Development

  • Rural Development covers the following areas Making arrangement of drinking water facility by constructing Storage Tanks, Pump House, Water Pipe Line and Boar Wells Village Sanitation Programme
