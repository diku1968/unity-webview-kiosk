Pen Knives & Nut Crackers

  • Pen Knives and Nutcrackers of Kutch have traveled the length and breadth of the country. People of Kutch prefer to present pen-knives or nutcrackers ro their relatives and friends living outside Kutch. The artisans who made swords during the royal familys partronge, are now producing the pen knives and nutcrackers.

    The iron bars are reduced to workable small pieces. Then the pieces are worked by using a forging machine and joined by using rivets. The jointed pieces have edges which are sharp ened on a grinding wheel. To avoid rusting they are plated with stainless steel. Nutcrackers in brass fetch the against price. Some time tiny bells are inserted inside the edge of nutcrackers to produce a mellow sound. The main centers of production are Anjar, Bhuj, Nana-Reha and Mota-Reha.
