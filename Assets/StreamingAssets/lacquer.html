Lacquer Work

  • Wool turning and lacquering on it is another traditional craft. Colours sticks are prepared by heating process by using button lac lithopone (zinc powder) and sort of colour used for textile dyeing. Teak wood or desibaval is used for the article coloured lac stick is pressed against its surface while it is kept revolving on the lathe.

    Due to the heat of friction the colour melts and spreads. Then apiece of a wood is pressed on to the surface and oil applied via a cotton rag for even application. Later, designs of flowers and leaves are worked by using piece of a cloth over the coloured surface. to produce a multi coloured effect lac stick of yellow, red, white and black colours are applied one after the other and the layers of unwanted colour are removed.

    Lacquered bed posts, dandia (Stick), Ghodiya (Cradles), Hand of spoon Velans (Chapati rolles) Bajots (low stool) patla, Flower pots and Powder boxes are produced at Nirona, Zura and Bhirendiara.
