MUDWORK PAINTING

  • House in the Banni area are living museums of beautiful paintings Rabari and Harijan women folk are treditionally experts in dedicating their dreams in paining. They turn houses in to homes. The clay collected is mixed with camel dung and kept for a few days. Than it is kneaded to obtain sufficient plasticity and designs are worked on the mud wall using this clay mixtures. Designs of mor (Peacock), Popat (Parrot), Anghadi (Fingers), Vinjno (Fan), Hathi (Elephant), Ghoda (Horse), Unth (Camel), Zad (Tree), Fal-phool (Fruits and flowers) and Vel (climbers) are depicted. to increase the aesthetic value, tiny mirrors are set in between the designs. After drying the wall is washed using white earth colour Bhujodi, Ghada, Ludia are the main centres of the crafts. Now adays walls of hotels and museums are decorated with these sorts of beautiful paintings.
